Dictate into any app using a floating microphone button or the MyWhisper keyboard.

<b>Floating button</b>
<ul>
<li>Appears over any focused text field and follows the keyboard.</li>
<li>Tap to dictate, then tap ✓ to insert or ✕ to discard.</li>
<li>Or press and hold to talk: release to send, slide up to lock, slide down to cancel.</li>
<li>Drag it, snap it to the edge, fade it, lock it, or snooze it.</li>
</ul>

<b>MyWhisper keyboard</b>
<ul>
<li>An input method with a mic bar: hold and release, or tap to start and tap again to finish.</li>
</ul>

<b>Speech recognition</b>
<ul>
<li>Self-hosted OpenAI-compatible HTTP and sherpa-protocol WebSocket servers, or cloud providers.</li>
<li>Experimental on-device speech recognition and text cleanup, off by default.</li>
<li>Text cleanup, custom dictionary, transcript history, and sync, all managed from the app.</li>
</ul>
