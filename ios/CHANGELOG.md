# Changelog

All notable changes to the iPhone app are documented here. Tags: `ios-vX.Y.Z`.

## [Unreleased]

### Added
- First usable preview, as an unsigned IPA for SideStore or AltStore. Dictate from the MyWhisper keyboard, which also types with swipe, predictions, auto-correction, emoji and English/Spanish layouts. The app has the same Activity, Providers, Dictionary and Settings tabs as Android, with history, stats, speech and cleanup providers, dictionary, snippets and LAN sync. Sync runs only while the app is open, and on-device models are not available on iOS yet.
